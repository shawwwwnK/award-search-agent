# ADR 0026: Model-authored Results structure with bound facts

## 2026-10-07 implementation disposition

The owner invoked implementation of the October 7 Results M2 spec. The local API now implements
that spec's selected checks, bounded correction and annotated delivery. See the
[implementation log](../build-log/2026-10-07-results-m2-implementation.md) and
[usage](../results-m2.md). Numerical model/live settings remain explicit, unselected inputs;
UTF-8 input measurements do not qualify context fit. This records implementation status and
routine engineering mechanics, not a new product-policy decision or owner qualification.
Historical unimplemented/unsettled statements below retain their original dates; the
[October 7 execution contract](../handoffs/2026-10-07-results-m2-validation-and-delivery-contract.md)
identifies policy supersession. Ranking ownership and upstream trust remain under ADR 0027.

- Status: Accepted ownership boundary; detailed engineering mechanics remain proposed
- Decision dates: 2026-10-02 Results design opening; structural control reaffirmed 2026-10-04
- Recorded: 2026-10-04 (backfill; Results runtime remains unimplemented)

## Context

Results must explain checked alternatives while preserving facts, conditions and limitations.
The earlier code-assembled editorial-fields proposal imposed answer structure. The owner required
LLM control of structure and reaffirmed it on October 4. Sources are the
[revised design](../handoffs/2026-10-02-results-stage-v1-design.md),
[authored-document contract](../handoffs/2026-10-03-results-stage-authored-template.md), and
[active opening](../handoffs/2026-10-04-results-m2-opening.md).

## Options

The documented alternatives are a code-owned answer skeleton populated with model editorial fields
and the selected model-authored document with bound factual placeholders. The former was replaced
because the owner requires model control over headings, order, grouping, paragraphs, lists,
tables, emphasis and prose. This records that owner requirement rather than an experimentally
proven superiority claim.

## Decision

The model owns visible answer structure and explanatory prose. Code supplies factual slots,
fills them from trusted upstream facts and checks final visible content without imposing layout.
Required facts and disclosures are content obligations, not fixed sections or cards. Code must
not silently append a corrective section, rearrange the draft or conceal a journey's conditions
in a generic warning. Invalid authorship yields an explicit failure under the eventual call policy.

Conditional requirements, separate-booking obligations, quote scope, missing costs, cabin limitations
and coverage must remain visibly associated with selected journeys. Direct cash stays a benchmark;
rejected records cannot become selections or model-promoted validated journeys. Ranking owns
membership and eligibility; Results owns presentation selection and explanation.

## Consequences

This supersedes the earlier code-assembled answer proposal, without reopening upstream validation.
Flexible layout increases the burden on scope, substitution and visible-content checks.
A filled document alone cannot prove the model's prose is grounded or the answer is useful.
Results M2 is active but runtime remains unimplemented; M3 evaluation remains planned.

The scoped-parts wire schema, strict Markdown subset, source maps, exact replay and repair mechanics
in the authored-document contract are revised engineering proposals. This ADR does not elevate
all those mechanics to owner-approved product policy. Writer/judge settings, fallback breadth,
numerical live budgets and authoring context fit remain unsettled.

## Evaluation

Planned offline checks must accept both prose and table layouts, including repeated/interleaved
journey scopes, while rejecting missing, hidden, incorrectly associated or altered facts and
conditions. Check final concatenated visible content and preserve source attribution through
substitution. Later bounded authoring diagnostics and calibrated advisory evaluation must assess
unsupported prose and task usefulness separately from mechanical slot checks.
The [design review](../reviews/2026-10-04-results-stage-design-review.md) is design evidence,
not Results implementation or qualification. No new live call is authorized by this ADR.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit if authored layouts cannot preserve visible associations, unsupported prose remains material,
or owner-reviewed tasks require a different authorship boundary. Settle schema and failure/call
policy during Results implementation, recording consequential accepted changes explicitly.

## 2026-10-06 amendment — M2 validation and presentation decisions

Status: accepted directions from the owner's grill-with-docs answers; validation/fallback
mechanics remain unsettled. Results runtime remains unimplemented. Decision and recording
date: 2026-10-06. Source: [interview record](../build-log/2026-10-06-results-m2-design-grilling.md).

### Context and options

The interview challenged mechanical slot validity versus semantic truth, scoped-document
association, context overflow, explicit generation failure versus factual fallback, the
proposed two-per-award-group restriction, and visible disclosure versus detailed provenance.
The owner requires that “the flow needs to flow” and that M2 converge on validation and
fallback. M3 is an evaluator providing output-quality metrics, not a delivery validator.

### Accepted decisions

- Adopt the selection-manifest/scoped-Markdown-parts format. When a journey scope resumes
  after another journey, require visible journey-identification facts; invisible metadata
  alone does not establish reader-facing association. The exact identification/association
  checks remain to be designed.
- Retain the five-complete-journey total limit; remove the proposed one-primary/one-alternate
  (two-per-group) cap. The model must justify materially distinct choices and avoid redundancy.
  Existing same-group alternate references may remain; they do not impose a numeric group cap.
- Preserve decision-changing facts and conditions in the answer, including quote scope and
  separate-ticket limitations. Identical shared disclosures may appear once when applicability
  is clear. Detailed provenance belongs in the artifact. Show component observation dates;
  add precise times when they materially affect interpretation. Other existing factual
  obligations are not removed by this decision.
- Clean irrelevant fields and factor the full input without silently dropping distinct
  alternatives or decision-relevant evidence. The owner expects capped acquisition and cleaning
  to make fit practical; this is an expectation, not measured context-fit evidence. Measure
  complete prepared input. If it still cannot fit, record an explicit context-limit outcome
  before changing authoring strategy. Its delivery treatment depends on the unresolved fallback.
- M2 owns delivery validation and fallback; M3 supplies output-quality evaluation and metrics.
  Mechanical slot checks alone are insufficient to settle M2's delivery-validation design.

### Unsettled conflict and consequences

The owner also accepted the interview's Q4 recommendation of explicit initial generation
failure without a factual fallback, which conflicts with the Q1 continuity requirement.
Do not infer that the owner chose either terminal no-answer delivery or a particular fallback.
The next interview round must resolve delivery behavior and the semantic-validation boundary.
A runtime semantic judge, repair loop, fallback selection policy and numerical call budgets
are not approved by this amendment. Retain failure evidence separately from any delivered answer.

This amendment supersedes the two-per-group proposal and the allocation of delivery validation
to later quality evaluation. It preserves LLM control of normal-answer structure, trusted
upstream decisions, eligibility, five-choice total, and separate cash-benchmark policy.
Earlier no-runtime-judge/no-repair proposals are unsettled where they would constrain the new
M2 validation/fallback discussion; no replacement mechanism is yet selected.

### Evaluation and revisit triggers

Planned evidence must exercise three meaningful alternatives in one award group, resumed
scope identification, shared versus journey-specific disclosures, and date/precise-time
freshness distinctions. Validation/fallback checks await their policy decisions. No runtime
checks, tests or live calls were performed in this design interview. Revisit if cleaning cannot
preserve complete input within context, association checks restrict legitimate layouts, or
M2 validation/fallback cannot deliver grounded continuity within agreed budgets.

## 2026-10-07 amendment — validation recovery without code-owned layout

Status: accepted continuity/authorship boundary; recovery and claim-validation mechanics open.
Decision and recording date: 2026-10-07. Source: [interview continuation](../build-log/2026-10-06-results-m2-design-grilling.md#round-2--2026-10-07).

### Context and options

Round 2 separated delivery continuity, semantic validation, and deterministic fallback layout.
The owner accepted delivering grounded fallback information with failure evidence retained,
then explicitly rejected code-owned fallback layout. They clarified that API failures are system
errors and that validation errors should not stop the flow. This resolves the earlier Q1/Q4
ambiguity for validation errors; it does not promise successful delivery during API outages.

### Decision

M2 must recover from validation errors rather than treat them as terminal no-answer delivery.
Retain invalid-attempt evidence separately from successful recovery. Recovery/fallback answers
remain model-authored in structure; a code-owned fallback layout is not permitted. API failures
are system errors. The precise recovery strategy, stopping rules, handling of other system/source
failures and budgets remain to be settled; no unbounded retry loop or invalid-answer delivery
is authorized by this continuity direction. M3 remains quality evaluation, not delivery validation.

The owner proposed structured claim kinds such as ALL_LEGS_BUSINESS and PROTECTED_CONNECTION,
with deterministic checks, and asked whether this preserves semantic freedom. This is a proposal,
not an accepted schema or evidence policy. Its claim-to-visible-prose binding, undeclared claims,
unknown evidence and recovery behavior remain frontier questions. A runtime semantic validator
was recommended in Round 2 but has not been accepted.

### Consequences, evaluation and revisit

This supersedes the initial explicit-terminal-failure recommendation for validation errors and
rejects the separately proposed deterministic-layout fallback. Existing no-repair/one-invocation
proposals must be revisited against recovery requirements; no replacement attempt count is settled.
LLM structural ownership extends to fallback. Planned evidence must distinguish validation recovery
from API failure and retain every invalid attempt. Unknown evidence must not be converted into
confirmed claims. No runtime implementation, live call or behavioral test accompanies this decision.
Revisit when bounded model-authored recovery cannot meet the continuity requirement, or when claim
metadata does not faithfully describe visible statements and implications.

## 2026-10-07 amendment — non-blocking checks and disclosed residual failures

Status: accepted check/delivery direction; exact claim catalog, warning placement, renderability
boundary and repair budgets remain open. Decision/recording date: 2026-10-07, following Round 3.
Source: [interview record](../build-log/2026-10-06-results-m2-design-grilling.md#round-3--2026-10-07).

### Context and options

Round 3 compared typed claims bound to freely worded spans with runtime semantic checking, and
bounded repair with terminal recovery exhaustion. The owner accepted span-to-claim binding and
the acknowledged residual wording/undeclared-claim gap. They chose selected deterministic checks,
a default unknown/unchecked path, and delivery with customer-facing failures after repair attempts,
rather than using validation or recovery exhaustion as a system-error gate.

### Decision

- Bind material claim-bearing spans to typed claims; retain model-owned wording and layout.
  Deterministic checks assess the declared proposition/scope, not arbitrary paraphrase truth.
  Residual undeclared or misleading wording is measured by M3; no runtime semantic judge is adopted.
- Implement a selected set of important claim logics. Unknown is the default where no applicable
  important check is defined, and is non-blocking; it is not evidence that a claim is confirmed.
  The owner also permits unknown results to pass. Exact representation of unchecked claims versus
  implemented checks with insufficient evidence remains unsettled. M3 findings inform additions
  to the check catalog; they do not automatically change runtime policy.
- Validation failures do not prevent results delivery or become system errors. After bounded
  model calls, deliver the results with unresolved validator failures stitched on in language
  appropriate for travelers. Keep failure/attempt receipts; annotated delivery is not a clean pass.
- The instruction to stitch on failure messages authorizes a narrow exception to the earlier
  prohibition on code-added visible content. The answer/fallback layout remains model-authored;
  failure notices may be attached. Exact placement, formatting and affected-claim association
  remain to be settled. This is not approval for a code-owned recommendation layout.

### Consequences and limits

This supersedes validation-as-a-terminal-delivery-gate, the proposed recovery-exhausted system
outcome, and the no-appended-content rule only for residual validation messages. It does not
convert unknown or failed checks into passes, or change Ranking eligibility. API failures remain
system errors. The model may attempt repairs, but an unbounded call loop is not adopted.

Structural inability to render (malformed document or unresolvable references), source corruption,
and treatment of contradicted prose beside customer-facing failures still require decisions.
Do not infer authorization to fabricate substituted facts, promote rejected candidates, or emit
unsafe markup from the non-blocking validation direction. Existing hard-check proposals below
are superseded as terminal delivery gates where they conflict with this amendment; concrete
handling and recovery remain unsettled.

### Evaluation and revisit triggers

Planned evidence must distinguish supported/failed/unchecked outcomes, retain unknown as unknown,
exercise repeated repair failures followed by annotated delivery, and assess whether warnings
remain understandable and associated with the affected statements. M3 must include unchecked
assertions, misleading paraphrases, and annotated failures in quality metrics. No behavioral
implementation, tests or live calls were performed. Revisit check coverage and warning policy
when evaluation finds repeated material false claims or misunderstood qualifications.

## 2026-10-07 amendment — initial checks, recovery and delivery mechanics

Status: accepted Round 4 decisions; unresolved edge handling is identified below. Decision and
recording date: 2026-10-07. Source: [interview Round 4](../build-log/2026-10-06-results-m2-design-grilling.md#round-4--2026-10-07).

### Context and options

Round 4 separated unimplemented checks from insufficient source evidence, local versus distant
failure notices, partially recoverable documents from no recoverable generation, and repeated
correction from bounded annotated delivery. The owner accepted recommendations Q13–Q17.

### Decision

- Record unchecked separately from insufficient_evidence. Both are non-blocking and neither
  establishes truth. Existing required source disclosures remain visible. M3 can distinguish
  missing checks from missing evidence.
- Attach residual notices to the affected journey or claim. Use shared notices only for shared
  failures. Notices use concrete traveler language and identify unreliable assertions rather
  than generic technical error wording. Code-added notices are the narrow layout exception.
- Preserve recoverable model-authored content; unresolved factual references/placeholders display
  “Details unavailable” and an associated notice. Never substitute another journey's facts.
  Responses with no recoverable document, such as undecodable output, are system/generation
  failures, not validation failures. The owner explicitly requires development coverage for
  schema-error behavior; accepting this policy does not claim it was tested.
- Start with five claim families: cabin (including all-leg versus journey-level evidence),
  connection protection (including separate-ticket obligations), price scope (per traveler/party),
  fastest/cheapest comparisons (supplied reference and limitations), and eligibility (conditional
  versus no unresolved requirements). Other claims remain unchecked; M3 informs future additions.
  These checks do not change upstream eligibility or infer absent protection/cabin/price facts.
- Permit one initial authoring invocation and at most one correction invocation. Send all detected
  failures together; recheck the corrected document, then deliver recoverable content with residual
  notices. On correction API failure, retain the first recoverable draft and its notices plus
  the API-failure receipt. No automatic model fallback or extra correction loop is adopted.

### Consequences and supersession

This settles the claim-family selection, unknown distinction, notice association, partial
renderability policy and application-call count left open in the preceding amendments.
The earlier proposed one-invocation/no-repair profile is superseded by at most two invocations.
Model, output, timeout and live-campaign numeric settings still await prepared-input measurement.
Repeated entirely undecodable output is a generation failure; residual check failures on
recoverable content never become system failures.

Handling known mandatory disclosure omissions, rejected/over-limit selections, and which
recoverable draft to retain when correction worsens checks still requires clarification. No
automatic source-policy change, hidden input pruning or code-owned recommendation layout follows.

### Evaluation and revisit triggers

Development evidence must exercise unchecked/insufficient distinctions, local notices in prose
and tables, unavailable placeholders without fact splicing, complete schema failure, five claim
families, at-most-two invocation accounting, persistent validation failures and correction API
failure with original-draft delivery. No tests or model calls ran in this documentation session.
Revisit notice placement if traveler interpretation remains misleading, or repair budgets if
measured failure patterns justify another explicit policy decision.

## 2026-10-08 amendment: measured live input and bounded Luna diagnostic

Status: accepted engineering decision within the owner-authorized Results M2 diagnostic.
Decision and recording date: 2026-10-08. The owner selected Luna, delegated output/timeout
selection, and instructed completion of the live diagnostic. This does not close M2 or qualify M3.

### Context and options

The existing UTF-8 byte upper bound blocks two complete inputs even though the model-specific
count endpoint measures 688,786 and 468,897 input tokens; positioning measures 217,190. Options
were to retain the conservative rejection, use an approximate local tokenizer, or retain the
provider's exact full-request count. Trimming alternatives or inflating the model's physical
context limit would violate the existing evidence boundary and was not selected.

### Decision

For live authoring, count the exact model, instructions, input and strict response schema using
the [official input-token endpoint](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/token-counting).
Bind each count receipt to the full request digest, including correction feedback and the prior
document. Retain the original byte estimates as distinct measurements. Enforce both the model
input ceiling and context window with reserved output and an explicitly labeled safety margin.
Counting failures stop generation rather than falling through to unmeasured authoring; correction
measurement failure preserves the initial recoverable draft. Count requests are not authoring
invocations and do not permit a third generation attempt.

Offline preparation keeps its conservative measurement. Exact replay checks the retained count
response and request binding without a network call; it does not independently retokenize or
authenticate the provider's count. Existing byte-gated artifacts retain their replay boundary.

This diagnostic uses `gpt-6-luna`, 16,384 reserved output tokens, 300 seconds per authoring
invocation, zero automatic retries, disabled truncation and `store=False`. The documented model
limits are 922,000 input tokens and a 1,050,000-token context window, with 2,048 additional tokens
of safety margin. These are run-specific settings, not a new runtime default. See the
[declared run plan](../../evidence/results-stage/m2/2026-10-08-luna/run-plan.json) and
[model documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna).

### Consequences and revisit

There is at most one count request before each of at most two authoring attempts. Extra remote
measurement can fail; its outcome must be retained separately. The model-specific counts establish
input fit for these exact requests, not output quality, provider freshness or bookability. Revisit
settings when source size, model limits, schema or output needs change. Actual outputs and residual
presentation/check limitations belong in the diagnostic record and owner walkthrough; M3 remains
separate. The amendment supersedes the unselected-setting statements only for this declared run.

### Live-discovered notice placement and subsequent diagnostic scope

The first actual answer exposed prose notices inserted beneath the following option's heading
when adjacent authored parts lack boundary whitespace. Rendering v2 limits that insertion to
the originating prose part; table delimiter handling remains unchanged. Rendering v1 stays
available for exact replay of saved historical artifacts. This chooses annotation placement,
not headings, separators or normal-answer structure. Original live artifacts are not overwritten.
Valid controls, cross-part tables and version-specific replay are required evidence.

The owner subsequently lifted the campaign generation-call ceiling. Additional diagnostic runs
may be made and must retain all attempts and usage; the runtime policy remains one initial
invocation and at most one correction per run. Explicit optional run guidance clarifies boundary
whitespace, already-supplied units and claim names without prescribing layout. `None` preserves
historical preparation. This is authoring development evidence, not a calibrated M3 campaign or
owner acceptance. See the [budget amendment](../../evidence/results-stage/m2/2026-10-08-luna/budget-amendment.json).

## 2026-10-08 amendment — rejected-candidate note disclosures

Status: accepted owner decision; corrected v3 implementation verified 2026-10-08. Decision and recording date:
2026-10-08. Source: the owner's acceptance, recorded by the parent, that full timing, cabin, cost
and booking disclosures apply only to recommended options; see the corrected
[active Results M2 contract](../handoffs/2026-10-07-results-m2-validation-and-delivery-contract.md#authoring-and-factual-obligations)
and [fresh challenge record](../build-log/2026-10-08-results-m2-disclosure-reconciliation.md#fresh-policy-challenge).
Implementation verification is recorded separately in the
[disclosure-reconciliation build log](../build-log/2026-10-08-results-m2-disclosure-reconciliation.md).

### Context and options

The authored template makes selection the condition for full recommendation disclosures. The
implemented checker instead required the full schedule, cabin, cost and booking-obligation slot set
for every journey mentioned, including unselected notes. The first October 8 reconciliation
incorrectly narrowed the accepted exception to rejected notes. A fresh decision challenge compared
the contract to the original template and identified this scope drift; the parent accepted the
review correction. See the [original template](../handoffs/2026-10-03-results-stage-authored-template.md#factual-slots-and-visible-obligations)
and the [fresh challenge record](../build-log/2026-10-08-results-m2-disclosure-reconciliation.md#fresh-policy-challenge).

The alternatives were to require full recommendation disclosures for every mentioned journey, or
to apply those full disclosures only to selected recommendations while requiring concise, exact
identity/status/requirements information for unselected journey notes. The review correction
restores the latter scope stated by the original template. No rationale beyond this contract
correction is attributed to the owner.

### Decision

Full journey disclosure obligations apply to selected recommendations. An unselected journey note
must preserve a stable visible identity, exact source status and its source requirements/reason.
When its source status is rejected, the note must also make its exclusion clear and retain the
source-grounded rejection reason; an admitted or conditional note must preserve that exact status
and must not be described as rejected. The owner-approved policy is that only selected
recommendations require the full disclosure set. Any extra preservation of conditions in concise
notes is a conservative engineering choice, not a separate owner requirement. Results cannot
promote a rejected candidate, assign it admitted styles or present it as a recommendation. This
does not alter Ranking eligibility, source records, the separate cash benchmark or the model's
control of ordinary answer layout. Benchmark and shared disclosures keep their existing contracts.

The separate shared-disclosure policy remains in force: an identical disclosure may appear once
when its applicability is clear; this permits omission from individual journey parts but does not
prohibit repetition or impose a global occurrence limit. The v3 typed `shared_disclosures` binding
is an engineering representation, not a separately owner-selected schema: a key is unique only
within each shared part, where it binds one fact to at least two distinct selected eligible journey
IDs with identical source-slot values and visible identifiers for every target. A valid binding may
recur in another part, and independently scoped occurrences may repeat the fact; each occurrence
is substituted once. Metadata alone is not evidence of applicability; ambiguous or differing
bindings do not earn shared credit. The implementation preserves exact v1/v2 replay semantics.

### Consequences, evidence and revisit

The v3 checker must distinguish selected recommendations from unselected journey notes, preserving
visible exact identity, source status and requirements/reason for the latter. Rejected notes must
remain visibly excluded with source-grounded reason; eligible unselected notes must preserve their
admitted or conditional status. Test concise valid notes in each status, missing/ambiguous identity,
missing status/reason, and full disclosures for selected recommendations. Shared-once tests cover
equal obligations with explicit applicability and differing or ambiguous applicability. Version
changed checker semantics so saved v1/v2 artifacts continue to replay under their original
contracts. The final eight-module Results suite passed 112 tests; scoped Ruff/mypy and exact v1/v2
artifact/legacy-fixture replay passed, and independent probes confirmed selected and unselected
contiguous-part behavior without raw `journey_id` slots. See the
[2026-10-08 reconciliation log](../build-log/2026-10-08-results-m2-disclosure-reconciliation.md).
G08 is complete for these engineering cases; do not claim full authored-document contract coverage
or semantic qualification.

This decision establishes the selection-based full-disclosure boundary and required concise
unselected journey-note fields; it does not establish semantic truth of arbitrary prose, full
traveler-task usefulness or M3 qualification. A known expressiveness gap remains: visible prose
such as “Options A/B do NOT have this condition” may not be checked as a negated proposition.
Existing checks do not determine whether such unbound negative prose contradicts a source; this
challenge does not authorize broader semantic-checker work. Revisit if owner review or M3 evidence
shows that the visible identity, status or source requirements/reason obligations are insufficient.
