## What M2 must achieve

Help a traveler understand useful award-led choices, their tradeoffs and what to
check next. The answer should read naturally, with exact flight information in
source-resolved cards. It should not require users to interpret internal states
or repeatedly read what is missing.

Keep these accepted requirements throughout the experiment:

- Preserve Ranking's actual time/cost/premium memberships and meaningful overlap.
  One journey may cover several perspectives. No new overall ranking, forced
  pair, fixed three-section layout or one-card-per-category quota.
- Give each visible choice a supported reason to consider it. A useful
  conditional program/date/cabin research direction is valid for incomplete
  results. Do not demand invented benefits or turn leads into bookable journeys.
- Show canonical route, schedule, cabin, program, points, taxes, connections and
  cash components in cards. Narrative can use a fact to explain a choice without
  reciting the card. The independent explorer retains all eligible exact variants.
- Use ordinary traveler language. The owner explicitly rejected “Potential
  cost-focused option—positioning fare unconfirmed.” Explain a practical
  consequence instead of exposing an internal classification.
- Explain shared uncertainty once; keep materially distinct limitations distinct.
  A specific fact repeated to support a different decision is not automatically
  a duplicate warning. Calibrate with the reviewed examples below.
- Preserve known reported numbers even when their pricing basis is unknown.
  Missing tax amount, unknown scope, party support and separate-ticket duties
  are different states. Different programs' point counts do not establish value.
- Keep the full origin-to-destination cash benchmark to one brief source-bound
  sentence. Positioning cash still belongs in its award-led journey's card.
- Luna owns wording, organization and ordinary numerical prose. The rejected
  inline comparison/value binding proposal must not return through this work.
- Keep selection plus composition, with one bounded correction only after an
  actual recoverable mechanical failure. No unconditional editor or extra call.
- No travel-provider/live FX calls, upstream policy changes, frontend milestone
  implementation, product multi-agent orchestration or M3 qualification.

## Proposed structural change

This is the final architect's recommendation, **not yet an accepted contract**.
Have an architect settle the smallest coherent interface before implementation.
Record an accepted change prospectively in ADR0029/current contract if adopted
under a new execution invocation.

1. **Prepare source condition identities.** Each identity retains actual
   field/state, provenance, role and applicable choices. Preserve c17's narrowly
   justified merging of award point/tax scope; do not merge conditions just
   because both are unknown. Derive display applicability from choices actually
   shown, not every member of the authoring pool.
2. **Let authored fragments declare what they explain.** A fragment may own
   multiple conditions, and a condition may have at most one owning fragment
   across the visible response. Fragments may appear in captions, opening,
   comparisons, the cash sentence or other prose. The model chooses wording and
   position. Choose the precise fragment/schema representation during design;
   this handoff does not mandate a field name or a fixed layout.
3. **Validate declared structure.** Detect duplicate ownership, unknown IDs,
   nonshown references, source-role mismatches and lost owning fragments.
   Preserve the existing disclosure policy: optional ownership tags must neither
   turn every source row into a mandatory checklist nor make required coverage
   optional. Distinguish valid omissions from missing obligations explicitly.
4. **Resolve and render without editorial rewriting.** Preserve text and source
   cards exactly through decoding, delivery, previews and replay. Do not silently
   delete or merge prose to obtain a clean checker result. Keep historical v1
   artifacts replayable; version new schema/check/repair semantics explicitly.
5. **Use the existing correction allowance.** A recoverable ownership defect may
   trigger the existing bounded third generation with frozen selected facts and
   actual findings. It must not add a fourth call or an unconditional review pass.

Unique valid tags cannot prove semantic correctness. Untagged text may repeat a
condition; a tagged sentence may explain the wrong thing; a numerically false
sentence may have perfect ownership. Independent review must inspect **all**
visible prose and cards. Do not claim semantic enforcement from tag validation.
