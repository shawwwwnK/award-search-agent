## Interpretation

An actionable conditional program-access reason can satisfy the confirmed
incomplete-answer goal without numerical superiority or an asserted traveler
balance. Date/cabin compatibility can likewise guide a research action when the
condition is intelligible and supported. A useful comparison can explain which
branch to investigate under which traveler condition; it need not rank the
branches against each other economically.

Q009's Alaska and Aeroplan captions explicitly offer conditional program access;
the United caption offers program access and a later-date branch. These partly
match the accepted example. The original C fail is therefore **interpretation-
disputed**, specifically where it treats program/date compatibility as inherently
insufficient. The answer could explain the research choice more clearly, and
listing two or several carriers without connecting that list to a practical
benefit adds little. A different date by itself is not automatically a benefit.
None of those observations establishes that the answer must select a same-program
pair or quote the minimum mileage within a program.

The January13 United and January24 Alaska examples in the original review remain
legitimate optional improvements. Their unknown scope and incomplete schedules
must remain explicit if used. No source-supported numerical comparison, preferred
date, cabin mix, lead count or independent ranking policy is now mandatory.

This adjudication does not independently regrade C or convert q009 into an accepted
draw. It records the disputed interpretation and recommends prospective reviewer
clarification against the already-confirmed owner goal.
