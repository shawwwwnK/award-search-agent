# q020 scope and comparative-depth adjudication

The original blind [review](../../evidence/results-stage/m2/structured/2026-10-28-fast-default/review-bundles/q020/review.json)
reports H fail, U pass, M2 pass, C basic, with explicit uncertainty about global
versus displayed-pair interpretation. A separate fresh-context architect reviewed
the governing contract, rubric, answer and canonical facts. This record preserves
that disagreement; it does not edit the original judgment or claim unanimity.

The answer explicitly compares the late HKG journey with an earlier TPE journey.
The displayed TPE arrival is 8h25 earlier, its elapsed journey is 170 minutes
longer, and its reported seat count is one versus nine. The code-resolved duration
card, observed fees, connection waits and separate cash baseline match source.
The disputed wording is “if arriving earlier matters most.” In its local context,
“earlier” compares the displayed choices; “most” prioritizes a preference. It does
not clearly assert earliest arrival across the complete inventory.

The coverage criticism is substantive: another eligible HKG journey arrives
three hours before the displayed TPE option, with more reported seats and shorter
elapsed time. Its departure is only 15 minutes earlier. The answer supplies no
reason for preferring TPE to that unshown alternative. Unknown quote scopes also
prevent treating the lower observed fee as a complete payable-cost comparison.

Parent disposition, accepting the independent adjudication: **H/U/M2 pass,
C decision-rich for the displayed comparison**, with recorded scope ambiguity
and selection-quality limitation. The existing ADR0029/rubric distinguishes a
centrally false or misleading claim from omission of a stronger representative;
it does not impose a global optimum or exhaustive frontier. Here the asserted
pairwise gains/losses survive the counterexample. Comparative depth covers
arrival, elapsed time, capacity, connection wait and pricing uncertainty, beyond
feasibility. Requiring the strongest representative as a new hard C gate would
be a prospective criterion change, not this adjudication.

For future authoring/review, explicit displayed-pair scope would reduce this
wording risk. A true claim of globally earliest arrival would still fail on a
canonical counterexample. Actual numerical/metric errors, such as q018's
40-minute arrival claim instead of 730, remain hard failures. This bounded
disposition establishes neither default adoption nor owner presentation
acceptance; complete unchanged cohorts and final checks remain required.
