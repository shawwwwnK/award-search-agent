## What the design now demonstrates against M2

M2's objective remains useful traveler decisions expressed naturally around
source-resolved flight cards. The final examples demonstrate much of that shape:

- **Ranking ownership and useful overlap:** selected witnesses resolve to actual
  source memberships, with optional additional choices and deduplicated overlap.
  The c15 indexed array prevents separately named witnesses from expanding the
  authoring pool beyond its bound. Code verifies indexes and source membership;
  Luna still chooses the pool and which useful members to show. No new ranking,
  overall winner, mandatory pair or one-card-per-category policy was added.
- **Cards and natural explanation:** exact route, schedule, program, cabin,
  reported points, taxes, cash components and connections live in cards. Luna
  explains supported reasons and writes ordinary numerical prose. The selected
  temporal and choice-fact aids support actual comparisons without binding
  clauses or forcing a numerical contrast. Incomplete leads remain research
  directions, with program/date/cabin suitability allowed rather than invented
  itinerary certainty or mandatory lowest-points choices.
- **Cash and unknowns:** one visibly labeled origin-to-destination cash sentence
  stays outside the award cards. Positioning cash retains its separate role.
  Provenance-backed condition normalization avoids representing one award-scope
  fact twice, while preserving distinct unknown amount, party and cash states.
  Known numbers are not made missing by unknown basis; incomplete quotes with
  known scope still do not establish per-person/all-in/cheapest conclusions.
- **Integrity and bounds:** source cards, complete reference population and exact
  historical requests/replays survive the experiments. Indexed selection and
  profile-specific schemas/checks address mechanical failures explicitly. Saved
  unsuccessful draws and the independent source gate prevent clean artifacts
  from being mistaken for correct or useful prose.

These are demonstrated capabilities and engineering evidence, not sufficient
acceptance. Repeated missing-information prose remains a failure of the user's
explicit natural-language goal, even with accurate cards. Useful reasons,
field meaning and comparison ownership have also regressed across previous
versions. The small optional pool has not been proven adequate for every source.
The independent explorer is retained at the source/reference boundary; no
interactive frontend or integrated workspace behavior was qualified.

## Learning across twenty iterations and the earlier thirty

The full [campaign table](../../evidence/results-stage/m2/structured/2026-10-29-category-led/README.md)
retains each hypothesis and result. The meaningful design lessons are:

1. **Represent enforceable choices directly.** C06 could omit premium without an
   audit; c07–c08 addressed style witnesses, but c14's independent witness union
   still overflowed. C15's one bounded array with indexed witnesses removes that
   multiplication structurally. This is stronger evidence than another cap
   reminder. Membership, index bounds and justified omission still need checks.
2. **Expose source relationships without claiming they control prose.** Temporal
   equality, selected-choice facts and condition applicability make important
   distinctions accessible. C13 nevertheless compared an omitted pool member as
   if shown. C18 conflated known reported points with unknown basis. Exact input
   and card bindings do not verify unbound model language, as V11–V27 already
   showed. Adding more facts or another editor is not a demonstrated cure.
3. **More reasoning and less input are tradeoffs, not guarantees.** MED composition
   produced useful complete answers but exceeded timing in c10/c12. LOW c13
   improved timing while regressing complete-answer semantics. The subsequent
   smaller pool and indexed selector coincide with acceptable c15–c20 scout
   timing, but changing selections, draws and cache states prevent a causal
   claim about the cap. The deep c14 review's counterexamples remain valid.
4. **Clarifying policy helps interpretation but does not establish consistency.**
   The program-unit clause, natural-decision cue and precise known-number/basis
   distinction corrected real ambiguities without new Ranking policy. C19's
   incomplete-known-scope guard was preserved after independent review caught
   its initial omission. Later passing examples support those distinctions;
   they do not erase qualified earlier H/C/M2 failures or prove causal fixes.
5. **Condition repetition persists beyond input duplication or placement ambiguity.**
   C17 removed a genuine duplicate source projection. C19 clarified local/shared
   responsibility; c20 clarified the whole visible group. Q064 still repeats a
   singleton whose input occurs once. Further reminders alone are now a weak
   next hypothesis. The remaining problem includes allocating explanations
   across the response, not just discovering which conditions exist.
6. **Confirmation matters.** C11 passed scouts but failed one of six confirmations;
   c12 passed source scouts but failed timing. Old V23/V27 likewise caution
   against selecting favorable draws. The frozen conjunctive gates correctly
   stop this campaign without qualification.

## One proposed next cut — explicit ownership of condition explanations

**Proposed only; not accepted, implemented or authorized as a continuation.**
If the owner later opens new work, prototype a condition-ownership extension to
the existing single composition response before another prompt campaign.
Preserve the current selected source facts, Luna wording, source cards and
flexible response order. Do not introduce another model/editor call.

The bounded interface experiment is to let model-authored text fragments declare
the exact source condition identities they explain. Each condition identity has
at most one owning fragment across the delivered response; a fragment may explain
several conditions and can appear within a caption, comparison, opening, cash
sentence or other prose. The model chooses the words and location. The source
preparer supplies stable identities with their actual field/state/provenance and
applicable aliases; the decoder intersects applicability with the actually shown
choices. Omitted pool members create no display duty. Ordinary choice reasons
and numerical comparisons remain free model prose, with no claim/value binding.

Code can reject duplicate declared ownership, nonexistent conditions, references
to nonshown choices, lost owning fragments or mismatched source roles. It must
not silently remove text, combine distinct conditions because both are unknown,
require a limitations section, or turn every table row into a mandatory checklist.
Required disclosure coverage must retain its existing source policy rather than
be inferred from optional tags. Availability guidance and the single benchmark
sentence keep their existing responsibilities. Any new recoverable structural
finding would use the same bounded third-call allowance, not an extra editor.

**This is not a semantic verifier.** A model could repeat a condition in an
untagged caption, attach the wrong tag, or write a false sentence with unique
valid tags. Exact ownership checks prove only the declared structure. Source
review must continue to inspect all visible prose, including untagged text,
for repetitions, omissions, known/unknown conflation and useful reasons. If the
prototype merely adds a valid ledger while untagged prose repeats q064, it has
failed its purpose; it must not be presented as solving the M2 gap.

Before any new live authorization, an offline contract prototype should preserve
historical replay and exercise q064's repeated singleton, q061's shared condition,
q059's known-number/basis distinction and q065/q066's passing flexible layouts.
Negative controls should include duplicate ownership across card/prose, omitted
pool references, unknown IDs, distinct tax-amount versus scope conditions,
positioning versus benchmark roles, and untagged repetition that mechanically
passes but is explicitly rejected by semantic review. Keep optional subsets,
Q3 reasons, exact cards and one cash sentence. A future live evaluation would
still need unchanged source-quality gates, full checked timing, fresh frozen
confirmation and actual correction evidence; offline hand-authored examples
cannot establish model compliance or latency.

The recommendation is a bounded author-interface experiment, not a guarantee or
a request to revive c21. It may require a prospective contract/ADR decision if
accepted because it adds output responsibility tracking. No such decision has
been made. No further deep-architect escalation is needed to record this stop;
if later implementation design remains materially uncertain, escalate before
committing to that interface rather than launching another prompt ladder.
