## Grounded c16 recommendation

**Replace one ambiguous author-guide clause with explicit program-unit meaning;
keep the rest of c15 fixed.** The actual writer request says:

> Do not force same-program pairs, a lowest-points rule, or cross-program economic ranking.

This groups an unsupported economic inference with optional comparison methods.
“Do not force” does not clearly prohibit using cross-program raw counts as an
advantage when the author elects to do so. The selector's instruction is clearer,
but selector wording does not govern the separate composer. The selected facts
already pair every number with its exact program and scope. Another redundant
unit field or a new selection schema is not needed to expose a missing fact.
This instruction ambiguity is a plausible contributing factor, not a proven
cause of either q048 or q051.

A bounded replacement should express the following in one coherent passage:

> Do not force same-program pairs, a lowest-points rule or a numerical comparison.
> Points belong to their named loyalty programs: a smaller raw count in a different
> program does not establish an advantage and is not a reason to favor or investigate
> that lead. Conditional program access, date or cabin suitability can supply the
> reason on its own. Within one program, reported quotes may be compared while
> preserving their scope and incomplete status.

This clarifies the existing source/economic boundary. It does not ban accurate
card numbers, require a disclaimer after every quote, prohibit useful same-program
reported comparisons, demand a positive numerical tradeoff, or assume the traveler
owns points. Do not create code-generated prose, numeric clause binding, a fuzzy
semantic checker, a new reason field, selected-pair quotas or a lower-points
selection policy. Different-program choices remain useful when their conditional
program/date/cabin reasons are supported. Monetary quote scope remains governed
by the existing unchanged guide.

Apply the replacement prospectively to the c16 author guide; existing repair
requests inherit that guide when an actual mechanical correction occurs. Keep
the indexed selector, three-plus-cash pool, LOW/MEDIUM/LOW efforts, all source aids,
output schema/checks, card preview and correction cap unchanged. Historical
requests/replay stay exact. Verify that on a fixed source/selection, selector
requests remain unchanged and author/correction request differences are limited
to this guide replacement. No test of a hand-edited good sentence is semantic
qualification: fresh source review must evaluate the actual generated answer.

This is one targeted response to the repeated observed rationale error, not a
new generic prompt ladder. Do not simultaneously fix q050's minor wording, change
effort, add a source table or alter benchmark choice. If the same semantic error
persists, preserve it and reconsider the mechanism rather than declaring success
from mechanical checks or lowering the review standard.
