## Recommended c06 hypothesis

Replace the author guide coherently to allocate responsibilities clearly:

1. Treat selected records as an authoring candidate pool. Show the smallest
   useful subset that preserves materially distinct selected Ranking perspectives;
   a category overlap can use one card. The reference ceiling is not a target.
2. Give each displayed card a distinct supported reason or useful conditional
   research action. Discuss a local downside only when it distinguishes that
   option. If the only downside is common uncertainty, explain it once for the
   applicable group instead of repeating or inventing a card-specific downside.
3. Keep actual facts in source-resolved cards and use numerical prose only when
   it explains the choice. Do not display extra siblings merely because they are
   in the packet or have a source-supported pair comparison.
4. Preserve natural conditional program/date/cabin research branches; no mandatory
   same-program pair, numerical comparison or new lead ranking. A supported shared
   limitation may cover multiple programs without implying equal prices.
5. Keep exact temporal facts, truthful category semantics, one bound cash sentence
   and existing source/quote/identity constraints. Comparisons must involve visible
   exact options; omitted pool records cannot become invisible comparators.

Keep selector instructions/input, LOW efforts, reference ceilings, output schema,
checker policy, c04 duplicate-cash check and bounded-correction mechanism unchanged.
Do not append another contradictory per-card requirement, impose a new card quota,
add a common-limit regex checker or introduce an unconditional editor.

Before scouts, verify that c01–c05 requests/replay remain exact and that selecting
a smaller visible subset preserves required category coverage and exact comparator
visibility. A useful c06 observation is whether q013/q015's repeated prose disappears
without suppressing material conditions or usefulness. Independent reviewers still
judge complete cards-plus-prose, and all existing factual/latency gates apply.
