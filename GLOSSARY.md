# Award-search Results

Language for presenting observed award-led journeys and their limitations.

## Language

**Results validation**:
Checks of an authored traveler's answer against selected factual and disclosure rules; failed
checks are reported and do not prevent delivery.
_Avoid_: Delivery gate, M3 evaluation

**Results evaluation**:
Assessment and metrics describing a completed answer's truthfulness, selection usefulness and
clarity; this is distinct from deciding whether to deliver that answer.
_Avoid_: Results validation

**Decision-changing disclosure**:
A fact, condition or limitation that materially affects which journey a traveler would investigate
or what they must check, such as unknown quote scope or separate-ticket obligations.

**Award group**:
Related journey alternatives sharing an observed award option; their cash components and
conditions may differ and can make multiple alternatives worth presenting.

**Declared claim**:
An assertion associated with a journey or comparison and identified as a particular kind of
claim for checking; its wording may vary without making the assertion's scope interchangeable.

**Unchecked claim**:
An assertion for which no applicable important Results check is defined; passing it through
does not establish its truth.

**Validation notice**:
A traveler-facing explanation of an unresolved Results check failure, accompanying the delivered
answer and identifying the affected information.

**Insufficient evidence**:
An applicable Results claim check lacks enough source information to establish or contradict the
assertion; this differs from having no applicable check and does not establish truth.
_Avoid_: Unchecked claim

**Annotated delivery**:
A model-authored traveler answer accompanied by notices of unresolved Results check failures;
its delivery does not mean all checks passed.
_Avoid_: Clean validation pass

**Recoverable draft**:
An authored traveler answer with usable content even when some details or assertions have
unresolved problems; these problems accompany delivery as notices or unavailable details.
