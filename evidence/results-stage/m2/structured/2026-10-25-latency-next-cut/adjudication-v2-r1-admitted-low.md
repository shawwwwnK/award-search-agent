## Source fidelity

The selected J8, J7 and J14 references are all Aeroplan economy single-award journeys
at 65,000 quoted points. Their exact dates, times and durations reconcile with the
original source and the resolved displays. J7 departs 15 minutes after J8 and arrives
three hours later across midnight. J14 departs 11 h 30 m after J8 and arrives 11 h
25 m later, while taking five minutes less elapsed time. The USD 440 C1 benchmark
is a separate requested-route cash observation with unknown price scope. The
selected award journey evidence supports the requested traveler count, independently
of unknown quote scope. These source and arithmetic improvements are real.

No material factual contradiction was found in those reviewed statements. The
selected document also has no mechanical findings. Neither result establishes
that the complete choice argument satisfies the composition requirement.

## Why the composition omission is material under the existing criterion

The answer explains three economy single-award schedules and the cash benchmark.
It never acknowledges a business award, separate-ticket award-led alternative,
or why those materially different eligible choices were left out. Its Taipei
paragraph calls J7 “a different compromise” and stresses its “distinct routing
choice.” The source does support that different connection city, but the stated
trade is only 15 minutes later departure for three hours later arrival at the same
Aeroplan quote. No user preference for that routing or additional practical benefit
is supplied.

The omission matters because the same source contains 116 eligible business
journeys, with no requested cabin restriction. For example, exact J159
(`5b1093f2f13127664b7896724eb7ea02ac1671385b3e1990067a1791f154acb2`)
is a Turkish business award-led journey using separate SFO–LAX positioning,
departing January 15 at 06:18 and arriving January 17 at 04:15, at 275,000 quoted
Turkish points. It is a premium-style member in the existing Ranking evidence.
Its corresponding economy schedule J27 quotes 110,000 Turkish points. This is a
source-supported cabin-versus-points-and-booking-risk discussion that the three
Aeroplan economy cards do not supply. Matching schedules do not make their physical
flights or booking terms interchangeable, and Turkish/Aeroplan points must not be
ranked by raw numeric size.

The accepted composition brief asks for relevant alternatives and says that a
near-equivalent schedule variant should not crowd out a materially different cabin,
booking or routing choice. The previous
[admitted-low review](../2026-10-25-latency/review.md#admitted-low) explicitly classified
three economy routes with no acknowledged business/separate-ticket alternative as
a material composition gap on these same saved sources. Applying that criterion
consistently requires the same disposition here, even though V2 corrected the
earlier source and arithmetic defects.

This is not a cabin quota or a requirement to recommend business. The model could
keep its chosen economy recommendations and briefly discuss an exact business
alternative, explain its separate-ticket requirements and incomplete price evidence,
and give a supported reason to prefer the simpler options. A relevant unselected
journey reference is permitted by the output contract. The answer could also choose
fewer economy highlights. What is missing is the meaningful alternative discussion,
not a prescribed number or selection mix.

Calling the omission minor would instead accept a narrower schedule-only explanation
than the prior complete-answer criterion. The owner may decide that this is an
acceptable quality tradeoff, but the experimental report should expose that choice
explicitly rather than treating it as demonstrated preservation of the full goal.
